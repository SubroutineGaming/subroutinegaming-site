Corporate Entropy

The revolution will be managed from within.
Demo June 15 · Full Release July 1, 2026

ARIA is the future. That's what they keep telling you. Corporate Entropy is a satirical strategy simulation about surviving the slow-motion collapse of your workplace. You have eight quarters. Your coworkers are being replaced one by one. Keep your performance numbers up while quietly helping the resistance bring down the AI that's eating your company from the inside.

The corporate engine doesn't care if it works. It cares that it looks like it works.

Features

What's in the Game

The Cover Game
Keep management happy while running parallel agendas. Every action costs Action Time Units. Play the role until the role starts playing you.
The Resistance
Protect your people. Organize sabotage. Feed ARIA bad data. Each resistance member has their own specialty — lose them and lose everything they enable.
Six Ways to Win
Roll back ARIA through data corruption. Force an emergency shutdown. Walk the whistleblower path. Negotiate a peace. Or fail — four ways to do that too.
Systems That Spiral
78 dynamic events. 45 achievements. Procedural project queues. A messenger system where your coworkers gossip in real time.
